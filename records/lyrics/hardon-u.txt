I put my heart on you,
You made it prosthetic,
Put it through the machine,
Put it up in your attic.

I put my heart on you,
And I stained your clothes.
Give you a little bit of realism,
Give you something that flows.

(No one knows what it takes to make your mark.)

This is love for eternity,
Pure and natural.
This is love,
Completely expendable.

I put my eyes on you,
You put me on the floor.
At least hint a pearl or two,
Or my eyes depart, whore.

I put my eyes on you;
I put myself in prision,
Already put myself in a place,
To have to make a decision.
