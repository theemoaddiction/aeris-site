Regeneration,
Water based paint.
Dirt of the evening,
Drains away.
My fuel and filter,
Satisfies me.
Step in to season;
Out of my car.

It’s going all night;
Everything that I can see is all right,
Like a lucid/dripping dream. If I could
Take a pill,
And plant a little patience by the windowsill,
I would let it go on growing (on and on and on).

Distant horizon,
Swallows the sun.
Cut repetition;
Bite my tongue.
