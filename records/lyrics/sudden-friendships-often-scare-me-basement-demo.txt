I don’t know what you’ve been drinking,
But I could sure as hell use some of that now.
Come and bastardize my window.
I cannot see you through fog and frostbite.
Cherry reeks of your intention,
But I don’t mind I sure do like the company.
I just want to take your picture,
Something to remind me when you’re not here.

I don’t want to get to know you,
I think I’d just regret it soon.
Sudden friendships often scare me,
Especially the one with you.
I’ve been counting all my footsteps;
Writing talleys on my knees,
Waiting for my waist to crumble,
And gently rest upon my wandering weakness.
