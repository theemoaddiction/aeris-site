PROPERTY MOISTURE AND MOLD EVALUATION

Residential assessment without unnecessary disruption.

Schrenk Residential Mold Assessment is a family-operated rural property service. We provide visual moisture evaluations and non-invasive residential mold assessments for homeowners, landlords, and property managers throughout Northern Illinois.

Each visit documents visible conditions, likely moisture sources, affected building materials, and recommended next steps. Laboratory sampling can be coordinated when appropriate.

PLEASE NOTE

This office does not provide emergency water extraction, structural repairs, or mold remediation services.

Active flooding and sewage events should be directed to an emergency restoration provider.

Assessment services

01

Visual assessment

Inspection of accessible interior areas for visible fungal growth, staining, condensation, and material deterioration.

02

Moisture screening

Non-destructive screening of walls, ceilings, floors, and other accessible materials where moisture intrusion is suspected.

03

Sampling coordination

Surface or air sampling when site conditions and the purpose of the assessment indicate that laboratory analysis may be useful.

04

Written findings

A concise written summary of observed conditions, probable contributing factors, and practical recommendations.

PROPERTIES WE ASSESS

Every building presents different conditions.

Assessment scope varies with the age, construction, occupancy, and accessibility of the property.
